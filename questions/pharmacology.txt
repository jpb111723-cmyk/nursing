Which is one of the core five rights of medication administration?
A. Right dose
B. Right insurance
C. Right room number
D. Right pharmacist
Answer: A
Why: The five rights are patient, drug, dose, route, and time.

What is the normal serum potassium range for an adult?
A. 1.5 to 2.5 mEq/L
B. 3.5 to 5.0 mEq/L
C. 5.5 to 6.5 mEq/L
D. 7.0 to 8.0 mEq/L
Answer: B
Why: Both low and high potassium can cause dangerous heart rhythm changes.
