In triage, which color tag is used for patients whose condition is life-threatening and must be attended immediately?
A. Red
B. Black
C. Yellow
D. Green
Answer: A
Why: Red = emergent; yellow = delayed; green = non-urgent; black = expectant or death imminent.

A victim of a collapsed building has extensive injuries and is unlikely to survive even with definitive care. Which triage category applies?
A. Delayed (yellow)
B. Minimal (green)
C. Immediate (red)
D. Expectant (black)

Why: Expectant victims have extensive injuries and low chance of survival.

A patient with a simple wrist sprain walks into the ED during a flood disaster. Which category applies?
A. Minimal (green) or non-urgent
B. Immediate
C. Expectant
D. Delayed
Answer: A
Why: Minor injuries can be delayed for hours to days and moved away from the main triage area.

A patient with a stable open fracture who can wait hours without threat to life or limb is classified as:
A. Minimal (green)
B. Immediate (red)
C. Delayed (yellow)
D. Expectant (black)
Answer: C
Why: Delayed victims have significant injuries but can wait hours without threat to life or limb.

In emergency department triage, which patient must be seen within 24 hours without increased morbidity?
A. Non-urgent patient with an episodic illness
B. Emergent patient
C. Patient in cardiac arrest
D. Urgent patient with a serious health problem

A triage nurse classifies a patient with a minor laceration as immediate priority. This error is called:

During a surge, the hospital discharges stable patients early to free beds for incoming casualties. This process is:
A. Reverse triage

D. Under triage

Doctors decide that scarce resources should not be used on patients with little chance of survival, in order to improve the survival of others. This describes:

A restaurant patron suddenly clutches his throat, cannot speak, breathe, or cough, and has cyanosis. What is the best immediate action?

How long can a completely obstructed airway be tolerated before permanent brain damage or death occurs?
