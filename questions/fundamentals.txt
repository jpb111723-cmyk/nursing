Which action is the single most effective way to prevent the spread of infection?
A. Wearing gloves for all patient contact
B. Performing hand hygiene
C. Isolating every patient
D. Using sterile technique for all procedures
Answer: B
Why: Hand hygiene is the most effective, simplest measure. Gloves are an addition to it, not a replacement.

What is the normal resting heart rate range for an adult?
A. 40 to 60 beats per minute
B. 60 to 100 beats per minute
C. 100 to 120 beats per minute
D. 120 to 140 beats per minute
Answer: B
Why: Below 60 is bradycardia, above 100 is tachycardia.

Which site is most commonly used to check an adult's pulse during routine assessment?
A. Femoral
B. Popliteal
C. Radial
D. Pedal
Answer: C
Why: The radial artery at the wrist is easy to reach and the standard routine site.
